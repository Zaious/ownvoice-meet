# Pitch
! 一分鐘自我介紹。每段結束停半拍。
Hi, I'm Alex. This is a small tool that helps people follow online meetings in a second language.
> 大家好，我是 Alex。這是一個小工具，幫助大家用第二語言參加線上會議。

! （slide：問題）
Captions are easy to find. Answering out loud is the hard part.
> 字幕很容易找到。難的是開口回答。

! （slide：做法）
So the app turns what you type in your own language into short English sentences you can read aloud.
> 所以這個 app 會把你用母語打的字，轉成可以照著唸的英文短句。

## 難念的字
! sentences = SEN-ten-siz · aloud = uh-LOUD

# Q&A
Q: Does it work offline?
> 問：可以離線使用嗎？
Not by default. It uses cloud services with your own keys. You can point it at your own servers instead.
> 預設不行。它用你自己的 key 呼叫雲端服務。你也可以改接自己架的伺服器。
