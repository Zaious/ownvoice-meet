# Pitch
! 一分鐘自我介紹。每段結束停半拍。
Hi, I'm Zaious, an independent researcher from Taiwan.
> 大家好，我是 Zaious，來自台灣的獨立研究者。
I read English well, but speaking it in a meeting is hard for me.
> 我英文看得懂，但在會議上開口說很難。

! （slide：起源）
I built OwnVoice Meet in two hours, on the afternoon of my JCDL 2026 demo.
> 我在 JCDL 2026 線上 Demo 當天下午，花兩小時做出 OwnVoice Meet。
It shows what others say with the meaning in Chinese, my script, and English for anything I type.
> 它顯示別人說的話和中文意思、我的講稿，還有我打的話的英文。

! （slide：重點）
It never answers for me. The words are mine, and so is the voice.
> 它不會替我回答。話是我自己的，聲音也是我自己的。

# Q&A
Q: Does it answer for you?
> 問：它會替你回答嗎？
No. I type what I mean in Chinese, and it gives me short English to read aloud.
> 不會。我用中文打我想說的，它給我好唸的英文短句。
Q: How much does it cost?
> 問：要花多少錢？
Translation cost about eleven cents for my whole conference day, with my own key.
> 我整個研討會那天的翻譯費大約 0.11 美元，用我自己的 key。
